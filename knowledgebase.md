## FAQs

**Can it answer calls 24/7?**
Yes. The agent answers on the first ring, at any hour, any day.

**Can it integrate with my CRM?**
Yes — HubSpot, Salesforce, GoHighLevel, Zoho and more. Every call is logged and summarised automatically.

**Can it book appointments?**
Yes. It checks live availability and books directly into your calendar, confirming with the caller by text.

**Can it transfer calls to a human?**
Yes. Routing rules are defined during the build — urgent calls go to a human with full context.

**Is it customisable?**
Fully. Every agent is built custom — voice, conversation logic, integrations, and workflows.

**What is an AI voice agent?**
Software that holds real phone conversations — answers, understands, speaks naturally, and takes action mid-call. Not a phone tree. Not a chatbot. A working front desk.
